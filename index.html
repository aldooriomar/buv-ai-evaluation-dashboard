Colour guide — supporting context

Use the colour to understand governance intensity, never as a standalone approval label.

GREENExample: summarise published papers for lesson preparation; no personal/confidential data, no material decision.
AMBERExample: an assured enterprise service summarises internal notes; a person independently reviews the output.
REDExample: AI ranks students for progression risk, processes sensitive data, or can take consequential actions.
RESEARCH CONFIDENTIALITYExample: unpublished manuscript or patent work in an appropriately assured research environment.
